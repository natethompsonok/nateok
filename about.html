Nate Thompson

Executive Creative Director

My approach to creative leadership belongs to the intersection of technical logic, narrative driven concepting, and experiential design. I began my creative career over two decades ago hand-coding and designing websites, a digital foundation that taught me how complex systems, user journeys, and digital engines scale. Over the past 15 years, I scaled that logic into multi-market live experiences, guiding teams and anchoring global pitches for world class portfolios including adidas, Nike, Lego, Airbnb, and HUGO.

Narrative & Empathy

I attended the Rudolf Steiner School in Northern Ireland, set up by a group of parents looking for an alternative education for children growing up during the troubles. Amidst the conflict of the era, the curriculum provided a vital alternative framework, focusing not on rigid structures, but on interpretation through storytelling and visual art. Each morning was anchored in narratives that explored human economics, behavioural drivers, and shared human experience.

Unknown to me at the time, this unconventional start instilled a deep capacity to look past surface friction to understand what truly moves people. Beyond shaping how I interpret complex brand briefs, this storytelling foundation developed my deeply empathetic approach to creative collaboration and team leadership. I view briefs not through the lens of rigid structural execution, but as opportunities to cultivate real human connection, guided by teams that thrive on mutual trust and shared narrative focus.

Music

Parallel to my creative career, music has been a lifelong anchor and a vital source of creative inspiration. Having released music, composed short film soundtracks, and DJed over the past 35 years, I look at physical environments through a multi-sensory lens where sound, rhythm, and structural space dictate a broader human atmosphere.

For me, brand experience goes far beyond a physical build; it's an ideas-led, sensory playground where the concept dictates the execution, not the other way around. My work might manifest as a brick-and-mortar space, a 10 hour live stream broadcast, or a piece of cultural IP.

Capabilities & Outputs

While the concepts are fluid, my day-to-day work usually boils down to four main areas:

  1. Creative Leadership & Pitching

    Leading multidisciplinary teams through the entire lifecycle of a project—from the first blank page to the live build.

    Structuring and delivering high-stakes pitches for global brands, ensuring the creative vision holds its ground commercially.

    Protecting the creative energy of the team, fostering a highly collaborative environment built on trust rather than rigid hierarchy.

  2. Spatial Design & Atmosphere

    Designing large-scale physical environments, mapping out how people naturally move through, gather in, and interact with a space.

    Directing the physical look, feel, and flow of an experience so it feels like a natural extension of an overarching brand campaign.

    Merging physical architecture with broadcast tech, ensuring a space looks as impactful to a live audience as it does to millions streaming it at home.

  3. Audio & Sensory Direction

    Using sound, rhythm, and acoustic logic to manipulate the energy, mood, and dwell time within an environment.

    Composing custom audio tracks and soundscapes to elevate pitches and deepen the emotional impact of a live activation.

    Treating audio as an invisible layer of architecture that dictates how a room actually feels.

  4. Cultural IP & Programming

    Developing ideas that can scale beyond a physical footprint—turning live events into long-form entertainment or broadcast series.

    Connecting brands to music, sport, and lifestyle subcultures in a way that builds genuine community rather than forced promotion.
