Narrative & Empathy

I attended the Rudolf Steiner School in Northern Ireland. It was founded by a group of parents who wanted an alternative education for children growing up during the Troubles.

At a time of conflict, the school offered a different way of understanding the world. Storytelling and visual art were central to the curriculum. Our mornings began with stories that explored economics, human behaviour and shared experience.

I did not realise it at the time, but this taught me to look beyond the surface and ask what is really driving people. It continues to shape how I respond to briefs, work with teams and develop ideas.

I see creative leadership as a collaborative process. My role is to give people clarity, earn their trust and create the conditions for good ideas to develop.

Music

Music has always run alongside my creative career. Over the past 35 years, I have released music, composed soundtracks for short films and worked as a DJ.

This has shaped how I think about physical spaces. I pay attention not only to how an environment looks, but also to how it sounds, moves and feels.

For me, a brand experience is not simply a physical build. It starts with an idea, and the form follows from there. That might mean a permanent space, a ten-hour live broadcast or an original piece of cultural programming.

Capabilities & Outputs

My day-to-day work generally falls into three areas:

  1. Creative Leadership & Pitching

    I lead multidisciplinary teams from the first idea through to delivery.

    I shape and present pitches for global brands, balancing creative ambition with commercial and practical requirements.

    I also work to create an open, collaborative team culture where people have the clarity and confidence to do their best work.

  2. Spatial Design & Atmosphere

    I lead teams designing large-scale environments, considering how people enter, move through, gather in and interact with a space.

    I direct the look, feel and flow of an experience so that it connects naturally with the wider brand or campaign.

    I also bring physical design and broadcast technology together, making sure an experience works for the people in the room as well as those watching remotely.

  3. Cultural IP & Programming

    I develop ideas that can live beyond a single event, turning experiences into content, formats and longer-term platforms.

    I connect brands with music, sport and lifestyle communities in ways that feel relevant and considered, without forcing the brand into spaces where it does not belong.
